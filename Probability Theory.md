## Addition Rule
P(A or B) = $P(A \cup B)$ = $P(A) + P(B) - P(A \cap B)$
p(A and B) = $P(A \cap B)$

Mutually exclusive $\implies$ P(A and B) = 0

## Multiplication Rule
$P(A|B)$ (Probability A given B) = $\huge{\frac{P(A and B)}{P(B)}}$ 
	Example:  
		Suppose in a class:
		- 40% of students are male (B)
		- 10% are both male **and** left-handed (A ∩ B)
	Then:
	$$\large{P(A|B) = \frac{0.1}{0.4} = 0.25}$$
	means 25% of the males are left-handed

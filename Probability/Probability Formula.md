### Letter Arrangement
$$\huge{P= \frac{(Total\:Letter)!}{(Number\:of Duplicates)!}}$$
Ex: COMBINATORICS
$$\huge{P= \frac{13!}{2!\times2!\times 2!}}$$
### Inclusion Exclusion
$$\huge{|A\cup B| = |A| +|B|-|A\cap B|}$$

### Mutually Exclusive
$$\huge{|E\cap F| = 0}$$ Mutually Exclusive

### Independent Check
$$P(A\cap B) = P(A) \times P(B)$$
if equal $\implies$ Independent. If, no $\implies$ Not independent

### Derangement Problem
$$\huge{\frac{!n}{n!}=\frac{(1-\frac{1}{1!}+\frac{1}{2!}-...\frac{1}{n!})}{n!}}$$
### Conditional probability
$$\huge{P(E|F)= \frac{P(E\cap F)}{P(F)}}$$
